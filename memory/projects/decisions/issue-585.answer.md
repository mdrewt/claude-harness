Decision 1: I accept your recommendation. Monsters, items, and currency are tradable on both sides.
Decision 2: I accept your recommendation. One-sided trades are not currently a requested feature. However, this rule alone won't prevent soft-locks from trading. For example, trading your only monster for a single gold coin. So additional checks might have to be implemented. Keep the checks relatively simple though. A solution that prevents 90% of potential cases with 0 false positives is better than a solution that prevents 95% of potential cases but constantly frustrates players attempting legitimate trades.
Decision 3:  I accept your recommendation. Initiation stays face-to-face only; the Social›Trades tab is where you EDIT/confirm/cancel already-open sessions. Additionally, only one active trade per pair of players at a time. No spamming the same person with a dozen different requests/offers.
Decision 4: I accept your recommendation. Received monster goes to the first open party slot, else to Storage; the outgoing monster's slot is freed by the swap.
Decision 5: I accept your recommendation. Assets lock only once THAT side confirms.
