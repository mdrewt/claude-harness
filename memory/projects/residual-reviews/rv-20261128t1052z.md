### Cluster 1 — a reconnect attempt always ends, and the subscription owner sees every subscribe call

type: feature · tier: hard
category: netcode-determinism · severity: MED
closes: R-np-d-ATTEMPT-WATCHDOG, R-np-d-STALE-TIMER-OVER-LIVE-LINK, R-st-3-SUB-SECOND-CALL
touches: client/src/net/connection.ts, client/src/net/connection.test.ts, client/src/net/connection.runtime.test.ts, client/src/prediction/reconnectPolicy.ts, client/src/prediction/reconnectPolicy.test.ts

Measured at `af826856`:

- `attemptBuild` (`client/src/net/connection.ts:1469-1478`) moves the policy with
  `onReconnectAttempt`, which returns a connected link unchanged
  (`client/src/prediction/reconnectPolicy.ts:72-75`). Its failure then goes through `onAttemptFailed`,
  which also does nothing unless the link is reconnecting (`client/src/prediction/reconnectPolicy.ts:108-111`).
- The only timer in the module is the rebuild timer (`client/src/net/connection.ts:360-366`), and the
  base subscription's `onError` only reports (`client/src/net/connection.ts:1245`).
- The test mock records the queries of the first subscribe call only
  (`client/src/net/connection.test.ts:270-281`).
- np-d's two wiring tests for `reconnectNow` and `continueAnonymously` (`connection.runtime.test.ts`)
  assume a pending rebuild timer survives the call. A fix that cancels the timer re-points those two
  tests, and the slice records that as its own change.

Criteria:

- **C1.1:** WHEN a reconnect attempt starts, THE SYSTEM SHALL arm a watchdog for that attempt. IF the
  attempt has neither applied its subscription nor reported a connect error or a disconnect when the
  watchdog fires (its socket opened and stalled, its base subscription errored, or its credential
  never resolved), THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL supersede the build, close its socket and count the attempt as
  failed, so the ladder climbs one rung and a rebuild is scheduled.
- **C1.2:** WHEN an attempt starts or applies while a rebuild timer is pending, or starts over a
  connected link, THE SYSTEM SHALL leave no superseded build with an open socket. A connect-error
