### 1. `a_territorial_ranged_wild_attacks_from_the_cheapest_tile_not_the_farthest` (game-core/src/tactics/ai_tests.rs, criterion ts-6b-6.2)

**Board:** the wild sits at (3,3) with `ranged_kit` (Light 10 and Heavy 20, both single r2–3). Its base speed is 30, so one stride is 2 points. Spawn is (3,3). P1 is at (3,1), distance 2 and not adjacent, so moves are untaxed.

**Territorial plan:** `[hit(HEAVY,P1), END]`.
- Both skills hit P1 from the wild's own tile (d 2).
- Heavy is chosen before Light.
- The tile key is `(None, cost, y, x)`, so the own tile at cost 0 wins.

**Aggressive plan:** the same, `[hit(HEAVY,P1), END]`.

**Skittish plan (the non-vacuity check):** `[mv(2,3,1), hit(HEAVY,P1), END]`.
- Skittish allows Heavy only, and its tile key is `(Reverse d, cost, y, x)`.
- One stride reaches every tile within Manhattan 2 of (3,3) except P1's own tile.
- `[Move1, Heavy, End]` costs 2+4+0 = 6 AP, which is affordable. `[Move2, Heavy]` costs 8 and is refused.
- Heavy tiles in reach and their distance d to P1:
  - (2,3), (4,3) and (3,4) are d 3 at cost 1.
  - (2,2) and (4,2) are d 2 at cost 2.
  - (3,2) is d 1, out of range. (1,3), (5,3), (2,4), (4,4) and (3,5) are d 4, also out of range.
- The best key is (Reverse 3, cost 1); between y=3 and y=4 it takes y=3; between x=2 and x=4 it takes x=2. Result: (2,3), which is 1 from the spawn and so inside the leash.

The test also asserts the Territorial plan differs from the Skittish plan.

**Kills:** `farthest = !exhausted && stance != Stance::Aggressive`. Under that break, Territorial gets Skittish's key over a tile set its leash does not cut (every one-stride tile is ≤2 from the spawn). It would then plan `[mv(2,3,1), hit(HEAVY,P1), END]`.

### 2. `a_tactical_win_that_levels_a_monster_up_recomputes_stats_heals_and_auto_evolves` (tactics_settle_tests.rs, criterion ts-6b-6.1)

The `levelling_win(edge_min_level)` helper builds a fresh `fixture()` and `world()`, then registers and seeds what `apply_evolution` needs:
- a `species_row` "id" index, seeded from the content species;
- a `pending_evolution_notice` table keyed on `owner_identity`, writable and unique.

It then builds monster 11 with `party_monster(a(), PAIR[0])` (species 1, level 5). It keeps the defense 250 and speed 200 overrides, so a skipped stat recompute is visible, and changes three fields:
- `stat_hp = derive_stats(species 1 base, row IVs/EVs/nature, L5).hp` (call it h5);
- `current_hp = h5/2`;
- `xp = xp_for_level(6) - 1`.

When `edge_min_level` is `Some`, it seeds ONE `EvolutionPathRow` through a path_id-keyed handle, copying the monster_mgmt_tests registration chain: `{edge 1, from 1 → 4, min_level, essence [], all gates None}`.

The battle itself follows the existing `a_tactical_ko_pays_exactly_what_the_one_v_one_funnel_pays_for_the_same_wild_bt9_6` shape:
1. A party of one starts a pack-1 battle on map 1.
2. Preconditions: u1 is monster 11 at the row's damaged HP.
3. The wild is seated in Light range at 1 HP with First-Stand spent, and the hit is forced.
4. The Light submit KOs it. Precondition: SideAWins.

Oracles:
- `gained` = the award `on_unit_defeated` + `pay` give u1, computed on `loaded(...).state`.
- `anchor_hp` = (current_hp, max_hp) of monster 11's side-A entry in the post-finish anchor.

**Scenario (a), no edge:**
- Precondition: the award crosses a level, i.e. `want_level > 5`.
- `(xp, level) == after_gain(before.xp, gained)`.
- Six stat columns == `derive_stats(species 1 base, IVs/EVs/nature, want_level)`. A precondition asserts they differ from the seeded columns.
- Precondition: `anchor_hp == (h5/2, h5)`, i.e. the written-back HP and `old_max_hp`.
- Precondition: `derived.hp > h5` (the `+lv` term guarantees this) and the monster was damaged.
- `current_hp == game_core::combat::xp::level_up_healed_hp(h5/2, h5, derived.hp)`.
- Species, and the `monster_pub` species, stay 1.

**Scenario (b), edge min_level = (a)'s post-win level:**
- The edge is unsatisfied before the win because level 5 is below it.
- Precondition: same `before.xp` and same `gained`, so it is the same deterministic win.
- Row and pub species == 4.
- `(level, xp, six stats, current_hp, pools) == game_core::evolve(monster_to_instance((a)'s levelled row), species_def(4))`, with essence `[0; 8]`.

Species 4 is the real target of content edge 1 (species 1 → 4, level-only).

**Kills:**
- Dropping the stat_* writes: (a)'s stat columns keep 250/200 and h5.
- Dropping the `level_up_healed_hp` line: (a)'s HP stays h5/2. A full-heal mutant gives h6 instead of h5/2 + (h6 - h5).
- Dropping the `check_and_evolve` call: (b) stays species 1.

### 3. `a_failed_write_back_with_a_fainted_member_adds_no_unfavorable_trust` (tactics_settle_tests.rs, criterion ts-6b-6.1)

**Setup:**
1. `live_pair`.
2. `fall_in_battle(u2)`: monster 12 faints and stays A's.
3. `ready_ko`.
4. Monster 11, the CONSCIOUS killer, is handed to `b()`.

`submit_queue` authorizes through `battle_unit.owner` (`sender_side`), not the monster's owner, so u1 can still submit.

**What happens:** `ko_submit` wins (it asserts SideAWins itself). `write_back_party_hp` then aborts on monster 11, so `hp_ok` is false.

**Assertions:**
- Precondition: monster 12 is `is_fainted()` in the anchor.
- Precondition: monster 12's owner is still `a()`.
- Precondition: monster 11's xp, level and pools are unchanged, i.e. nothing was paid.
- Asserted value: monster 12's `trust_unfavorable_count` is unchanged.

**Kills:** the `hp_ok = false` arm calling `apply_faint_penalties`. That function skips the non-fainted monster 11 before its owner check and adds +1 to monster 12. This is why monster 11, not monster 12, is the one handed away: handing away 12 would make the mutant `Err` on the owner check and survive.
