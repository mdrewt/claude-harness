AMBIGUITIES IN THE CONTRACT
  - When the outcome edge starts: at the show frame itself, or at the next syncStack. The tests tolerate either; they check the grace at show+399 and the cap at S+CAP-1 / S+CAP+1, with a frame at S+1.
  - The tests assume the outcome frame ([world, battleView]) is already on the stack during the wait, as the brief's "still on the stack" implies.
  - Whether the results show right on the skip key or only at the next frame. The tests assert only "by the next frame".
  - A Finished batch whose only new events are 0-cap (Awards) makes a 0 ms silent beat. Playback.playing() stays true until the next tick, so "nothing playing → results at once" is false for it. The contract does not say whether that should wait one frame.
  - The brief listed only the clock-fixture and depsOf changes. The four Finished-with-events changes (items 1, 4, 5) were not foreseen.
