## 1. Problem / intent

Drew's r2 directive is unambiguous and binding, unlike the 2026-07-25 amendment it supersedes: fusion is removed as a feature, not repaired. Evolution becomes a real, public, subscribable directed graph — species carry a standardized `tier: u8` (0–5, monotonic, never decreases — **PROVISIONAL cap, revised 2026-08-02 from an initial 0–3 against a real full-roster target of ~500 species / 7–9 tiers; see §4**), and `EvolutionPath` edges are AND-combined gates over level, per-Affinity essence balances, and two new history stats (Trust, Quality Time) plus the existing EV pool relabeled Nutrition. Essence reuses the existing 8-variant `Affinity` enum directly — no new taxonomy. A monster earns essence from wild-battle wins (typed by the **defeated** species' Affinity; practice and PvP battles are both exempt, §2 EG2-7), a new slow player-directed `essence_train()` action, or a crystalized-essence item. Evolution is always an explicit `evolve(monster_id, to_species)` call — never automatic — and when multiple paths are simultaneously eligible, the player picks. All 8 essence pools zero on every successful evolution. Bond is retired entirely, its two roles (investment accumulator, evolution gate) absorbed by a new Bayesian-smoothed Trust stat. Quality Time accrues only through existing player-triggered reducers, never a scheduled tick — a hard, cross-validated constraint (`evals/no-idle-accrual.eval.mjs` Check B). Nutrition needs zero new storage — it is the existing EV system, exposed as a percentage.

`fuse()`, the `Fusion` table, `fusion.ron`, and A0's taxed-carry math (ADR-0147) are deleted outright, not repurposed — the charter's caution against inventing a "combine two same-species monsters" mechanic gratuitously is respected; nothing in Drew's directive asks for one. This milestone is schema-touching and HEAVY (redesign + schema + economy-balance, all three risk-promotion triggers hit at once); §2 below lays out a five-slice, contract-first build order so client and content work can build in parallel behind a frozen schema/type freeze — matching how the sibling ceremony (`M-postgate-evolution-fusion-hardening`) sliced its own schema-touching work.

## 2. Slices, acceptance criteria (EARS) + located evidence (verified live, 2026-08-02)

### EG1 — HIGH: schema & core-type freeze (serial, ships first, blocks EG2–EG4)

Evidence: `pub_from_monster(m: &Monster) -> MonsterPub` (`server-module/src/marshal.rs:183`) is today a pure, DB-free projection with no species lookup, called at 14 `Monster→MonsterPub` sites today (`evolution.rs`×2 — `evolve()` and `fuse()` — `battle.rs`×2, `monster_mgmt.rs`×2, `movement.rs`, `content.rs`, `pvp.rs`, `raising.rs`×3, `taming.rs`, `trading.rs`); `fuse()`'s call site is deleted outright by EG1-9 (not migrated), leaving the 13 sites EG1-8 below actually updates. `EvolutionTrigger`/`EvolutionCondition`/`SpeciesEvolutions`/`FusionRecipe` live in `game-core/src/content.rs:220-280`; `resolve_evolution`/`evolves_to` live in `game-core/src/evolution/eligibility.rs:88-112`; `evolve`/`fuse` (the pure transforms) live in `game-core/src/evolution/transform.rs:32-104`.

- **EG1-1:** Migration A (additive-only, ships with this slice, per ADR-0006's additive-first discipline — `docs/research/spacetimedb.md` confirms automatic migration forbids adding columns without defaults, so every new column below carries one) SHALL add to `Monster` (private, `server-module/src/schema.rs`), each appended at the end of the struct with an explicit `#[default(0)]`: the 8 flat `u32` essence columns (`essence_fire`..`essence_dark`, saturating_add on grant, soft cap ~999, never reject), `trust_favorable_count: u32`, `trust_unfavorable_count: u32`, `trust_favorable_battle_day_epoch: u32`, `quality_time_ticks_total: u32`, `quality_time_accum_ms: u32`, `quality_time_window_ms: u32`, `quality_time_window_start_ms: i64`, `last_essence_train_at_ms: i64`. `bond: u8` and `evolves_to: Option<u32>` SHALL remain present and untouched by Migration A — their removal is Migration B, a separate incremental migration (ADR-0006's ADR-gated escape hatch) landing only after EG2/EG3/EG4 no longer read/write them (see EG5-6). Migration A and Migration B are never combined into one `spacetime publish` (automatic migration rejects simultaneous additive+removal changes to the same table).
- **EG1-2:** Migration A SHALL also add to `MonsterPub` (public), each appended at the end of the struct with an explicit `#[default(...)]`: `tier: u8` (`#[default(0)]`), the 8 essence columns (exact, public, `#[default(0)]` each), `trust_tier: TrustTier` (defaulted to its lowest variant), `quality_time_tier: u8` (`#[default(0)]`), `nutrition_pct: u8` (`#[default(0)]`). `bond: u8`/`evolves_to: Option<u32>` (both sides of the existing dual-write pair) SHALL remain present and untouched by Migration A; their removal is Migration B (see EG1-1, EG5-6) — never combined with Migration A in the same `spacetime publish`.
- **EG1-3:** Migration A SHALL add `Species.tier: u8` (`#[serde(default)]` for RON content authoring, `#[default(0)]` for the BSATN schema column — every currently wild-catchable species defaults to `tier: 0`) and SHALL author an **explicit, non-default** `tier: 1` entry for every currently-shipped derived species: **4, 5, 6, 9, 10, 22, 23, 30, 31** (proof-of-teeth: a content fixture asserting all 9 ids carry an explicit tier entry, not a serde default — skipping this makes every migrated edge fail tier-monotonicity simultaneously at first validation, a real bug independently caught by two review candidates). No species content SHALL author `tier > 5` (R11, §5 — PROVISIONAL cap, see §4).
- **EG1-4:** THE migration SHALL add a new public table `evolution_path` (`path_id: u64` PK auto_inc — a DB-internal mechanical key ONLY, never durable across content republish, NEVER referenced by client code, persisted gameplay state, or any future feature; `edge_id: u32` — author-assigned in content, stable and append-only exactly like `species_id`, THE durable identity for this edge; `from_species`, `to_species`, `min_level: u8`, `essence: Vec<EssenceRequirementRow>` — a `SpacetimeType`, precedented by `EncounterRow.entries` — `min_trust_tier: Option<TrustTier>`, `min_quality_time_tier: Option<u8>`, `min_nutrition_pct: Option<u8>`) — the full graph as a real public table, not an in-process RON cache, so the client can render the always-on requirements/progress panel with no server round-trip. A `#[index(btree)]` on `from_species` (or a composite unique index on `(from_species, to_species)`, doubling as DB-level enforcement of R1 alongside the offline content-integrity gate) SHALL be added — this project's own established convention indexes every other lookup-heavy table (e.g. `opponent_identity`, ADR-0109), and `evolution_path` heading toward 1,500–4,000+ rows at full ~500-species roster scale (up to 10 outgoing edges per non-top-tier species) makes the omission in the first draft of this table a real gap, not a stylistic one.
- **EG1-5:** `game-core/src/content.rs` SHALL gain `EssenceRequirement { affinity: Affinity, amount: u32 }`, `EvolutionPath` (the fields of EG1-4 minus `path_id` — i.e. INCLUDING the content-authored `edge_id`), and `TrustTier` (`Wary`/`Neutral`/`Trusting`/`Devoted`, `Ord`-derived) — REPLACING `EvolutionTrigger`/`EvolutionCondition`/`SpeciesEvolutions`/`FusionRecipe` entirely (deleted, not repurposed).
- **EG1-6:** `game-core/src/evolution/eligibility.rs` SHALL gain a pure `eligible_evolution_paths(instance: &MonsterInstance, paths: &[EvolutionPath]) -> Vec<usize>` — REPLACING `resolve_evolution`/`evolves_to` — and SHALL factor its per-edge, 5-gate evaluation (tier/level/essence/Trust/Quality-Time) into a separate pure helper, `path_satisfied(instance: &MonsterInstance, path: &EvolutionPath) -> bool`, that `eligible_evolution_paths` calls once per candidate edge. `evolve()` (EG2-1) SHALL call this SAME `path_satisfied` helper directly on its one targeted row rather than re-deriving equivalent logic — one shared predicate for both the "what are my options" read path and the "is this one choice valid" write path, so they cannot drift out of sync. `eligible_evolution_paths` itself is UNCHANGED in behavior (still returns the full eligible set, still powers the requirements/progress panel and the multi-choice UX, EG2-2/EG4-2) — this criterion only extracts its internals, it does not narrow what it computes. `eligible_evolution_paths` ALSO gains three pure helpers (`trust_tier_of(fav, unfav) -> TrustTier`, `quality_time_tier_of(ticks) -> u8`, `nutrition_pct_of(evs: &EVs) -> u8`) that `eligible_evolution_paths` calls internally. **All three tiers SHALL be derived internally from `MonsterInstance` fields** — none SHALL be an explicit caller-computed argument (the design's own first draft split this inconsistently across three structurally identical inputs; this criterion closes that). `trust_tier_of` SHALL apply Drew's specified Bayesian smoothing before any tier-boundary lookup: `smoothed = (fav + K) / (fav + unfav + 2*K)` with `K = 10` fixed (NOT playtest-tunable, unlike the tier-boundary numbers themselves — pre-seeding the ratio so a monster with a small action count doesn't swing to an extreme tier on one event, Drew's explicit directive) — NOT a raw `fav / (fav + unfav)` ratio. Proof-of-teeth: a fixture at `fav=1, unfav=0` SHALL resolve to `Neutral`, not `Devoted` (the un-smoothed ratio of `1.0` would wrongly saturate on a single sample; the `K=10`-smoothed ratio of `11/21 ≈ 0.52` does not).
- **EG1-7:** `MonsterInstance` (`game-core/src/monster/types.rs`) SHALL gain `essence: [u32; 8]`, `trust_favorable_count: u32`, `trust_unfavorable_count: u32`, `quality_time_ticks_total: u32` and SHALL lose `bond: Bond`. Field names SHALL match the schema's Trust columns EXACTLY (`trust_favorable_count`/`trust_unfavorable_count` — not `trust_favorable`/`trust_unfavorable`, a drift the first design draft introduced and this codebase's EV/IV cross-boundary naming symmetry does not tolerate).
- **EG1-8 (blast-radius, proof-of-teeth):** `pub_from_monster`'s signature SHALL change to `pub_from_monster(m: &Monster, tier: u8) -> MonsterPub`. ALL 13 call sites SHALL be updated in this slice: 2 gain a fresh species-lookup tier source (monster-creation in `taming.rs`/`monster_mgmt.rs`, already fetching `species_row`; and `evolution.rs`'s `evolve()`, already fetching the target species' gating data); 11 gain a copy-forward tier source from the existing `monster_pub` row (`battle.rs`×2, `movement.rs`, `content.rs`, `pvp.rs`, non-creation `raising.rs` calls, `trading.rs`) — EXCEPT `trading.rs`'s `confirm_trade` ownership-transfer loop (`trading.rs:643-655`), which currently has no existing `monster_pub` read to copy forward from and SHALL gain one new `ctx.db.monster_pub().monster_id().find(xfer.monster_id)` read per transferred monster. A missed call site fails the build (compiler-enforced); the test suite SHALL assert all 13 sites compile against the new signature.
- **EG1-9:** `game_core::fuse()`, `fusion_eligible`, `MIN_FUSION_BOND`, `MIN_FUSION_LEVEL`, `scale_u32` (ADR-0147's entire taxed-carry math), `FusionRecipe`, `fusion.ron`, the server `fuse` reducer, and its fuse-parity test matrix in `evolution_tests.rs` SHALL be deleted outright in this slice. The `Fusion` TABLE struct itself (a live schema element, unlike the pure-code deletions above) is exempt from this slice's deletion — table removal is forbidden by SpacetimeDB automatic migration (`docs/research/spacetimedb.md`) exactly like column removal, so it is left in place, unused, and removed in Migration B alongside `bond`/`evolves_to` (EG1-1/EG1-2, EG5-6).
- **EG1-10:** validation rules R1–R12 (§5) SHALL land in this slice, runnable against a placeholder/empty `evolution_path` content set (real content authoring is EG3, not this slice).
- **EG1-11 (exit criterion, proof-of-teeth):** the workspace SHALL compile with every existing evolution/fusion test updated for the new shapes; all 14 new `Monster`/`MonsterPub` columns (EG1-1/EG1-2) SHALL be verified appended-at-end with explicit `#[default(...)]` (per this project's verified BSATN additive-schema rule — mid-struct insert and nested-type widening without a default are rejected by live spacetime 2.6.0); `bsatn-compat-smoke.eval.mjs` SHALL be extended to cover the new columns AND the new nested `EssenceRequirementRow` `SpacetimeType` struct (a `battle-schema-snapshot.eval.mjs`-style table/shape freeze alone does not catch drift on `SpacetimeType`-derived nested structs, per that eval's own documented exclusion); `spacetime-type-snapshot.eval.mjs` SHALL gain a pinned snapshot for `EssenceRequirementRow`; and a schema-snapshot pin (mirroring the existing `battle-schema-snapshot.eval.mjs` pattern) SHALL freeze the `evolution_path` table + `MonsterPub` shape, INCLUDING the EG1-4 index and the `edge_id` field, and a test SHALL assert `evolve()`'s targeted lookup (EG2-1) and `eligible_evolution_paths` (EG1-6) share the `path_satisfied` predicate rather than each implementing their own gate logic — together, the contract EG2/EG3/EG4 build behind without further schema edits.
- **EG1-12 (durability contract, added 2026-08-02 post-ceremony review):** `path_id` (the DB auto_inc PK) SHALL NEVER be persisted into any gameplay-facing field, logged for player-visible purposes, or assumed stable across a content republish — nothing in `sync_content`'s upsert mechanics guarantees it survives a republish unchanged, unlike `species_id`'s explicit stability convention. `edge_id` (EG1-4/EG1-5) is THE durable identity for an edge; content authoring (EG3) SHALL assign it per entry, and the content-integrity gate (EG5-1) SHALL validate it unique and — like `species_id` — append-only (never reused, never renumbered across content revisions). When a future feature needs to reference "which edge a monster evolved via" (e.g. a deferred lineage/flavor field, §6), it SHALL be built against `edge_id`, never `path_id`. **Contingency, not a current concern:** `evolve(monster_id, to_species)`'s sufficiency (EG2-1) depends on R1 (§5) holding — if R1 is ever relaxed to permit more than one edge between the same `(from_species, to_species)` pair, `evolve()`'s signature must be revisited (promote `edge_id` to a wire-level parameter at that time); low-probability, nothing in this spec plans such a relaxation, but worth this one documented sentence so the assumption isn't silently forgotten.

No content, no client.

### EG2 — HIGH: reducers (essence grant, evolve rewrite, Trust/Quality-Time triggers)

Evidence: `care()`/`train()` live in `server-module/src/raising.rs:69/142`; `write_back_battle_results` lives in `server-module/src/battle.rs:1004`; `evolve`/`fuse` reducers live in `server-module/src/evolution.rs`.

- **EG2-1:** `evolve(ctx, monster_id, to_species: u32)` SHALL find+`require_owner`+battle-guard+trade-escrow-guard the monster (unchanged guard shape) → marshal → look up the ONE `evolution_path` row via the EG1-4 index on `(from_species=instance.species_id, to_species)` (REJECT "no such evolution" if no row matches — this is a normal, expected rejection, e.g. player error or stale client state, not a server bug) → evaluate that ONE row's gates via `path_satisfied` (EG1-6) — freshly, server-side, on every call; nothing about eligibility is ever cached, pre-computed, or trusted from what the client asserted it saw on the requirements panel — REJECT naming the specific failing requirement if not satisfied → call the unchanged pure `game_core::evolve` transform → zero all 8 essence fields and set `MonsterPub.tier` from a FRESH species lookup of the target (per EG1-8's fresh-lookup sourcing pattern) → dual-write. Trust and Quality-Time fields SHALL NOT be reset (lifetime history, not "spent" the way essence is). This SHALL evaluate only the one matched edge's gates, NOT the full `eligible_evolution_paths` set for the monster's current species — at up to 10 outgoing edges per species (full-roster scale), re-deriving and discarding 9 irrelevant edges' worth of gate checks on every evolve() call is pure waste once a targeted, indexed lookup is available; `eligible_evolution_paths` remains the correct (and unchanged) tool for the panel/multi-choice query, which genuinely needs the full set.
- **EG2-2 (proof-of-teeth):** WHEN two `EvolutionPath`s are simultaneously eligible for the same monster, `eligible_evolution_paths` SHALL return BOTH indices, not a single first-match winner — a positive pinned fixture with two concurrently-satisfied paths sharing one `from_species` (directly avoids the Tamagotchi/Wurmple/1997-Digivice "silent race" anti-pattern).
- **EG2-3:** `essence_train(ctx, monster_id, affinity: Affinity)` SHALL be new: ownership+battle-guard+trade-escrow guard (mirrors `care`); cooldown-gated via `last_essence_train_at_ms` (proposed 5h, playtest-tunable); grants a flat amount (proposed 5) of the player-chosen `affinity` via `saturating_add`; calls `accrue_quality_time`.
- **EG2-4:** `consume_crystalized_essence(ctx, monster_id, item_id)` SHALL be new — B2's reducer-shape successor: FULL guard set (ownership, both-role battle guard, monster trade-escrow, AND item trade-escrow — the item-escrow guard is non-optional; an earlier design draft omitted it, a real double-spend-shaped gap this criterion closes), PLUS the SAME cooldown gate `essence_train` uses (`last_essence_train_at_ms` — item-consumption and training share one clock, so a player cannot chain-consume purchased items with zero time-gating; no new storage needed). Decision-before-consume: REJECT unless `ItemDef.essence_affinity.is_some()` AND the shared cooldown has elapsed, with the item NOT consumed on either reject → `consume_one(item_id)` → grant `essence_amount` of `essence_affinity` via `saturating_add` → update `last_essence_train_at_ms`.
- **EG2-5:** `care(ctx, monster_id)` SHALL increment `trust_favorable_count` (replacing the deleted `bond += 5` write), change-triggered-recompute `MonsterPub.trust_tier`, and call `accrue_quality_time`.
- **EG2-6:** `train(ctx, monster_id, food_item_id)` SHALL remain MECHANICALLY UNCHANGED as the sole EV/Nutrition-granting path (no new EV logic — it already matches the binding constraint), gaining only a change-triggered `MonsterPub.nutrition_pct` recompute and an `accrue_quality_time` call.
- **EG2-7:** `write_back_battle_results` SHALL, on a wild-battle win (non-practice, non-PvP), grant `max(1, loser_bst / 30)` essence of the **defeated** species' Affinity to each winning active-participant monster (typed by the loser, not the winner — the "go fight the type you need" strategy; the divisor is deliberately 3x steeper than `battle_currency_reward`'s `loser_bst / 10` — essence is a slower evolution-pacing resource, not a currency, and reusing currency's exact rate cleared every authored threshold in 3-5 wins, a real undertuning risk); on a wild-battle faint (non-practice, non-PvP), increment `trust_unfavorable_count`; on a wild-battle win, credit Trust-favorable capped once/monster/rolling-24h via `trust_favorable_battle_day_epoch`; call `accrue_quality_time` for winning participants. ALL THREE (essence grant, Trust-credit, Quality-Time-credit) SHALL be BOTH practice-battle- AND PvP-battle-exempted (wild battles only) — the PvP exemption closes a collusion-farming vector two colluding accounts could otherwise exploit via repeated `challenge_pvp` rematches (no rematch cooldown exists in `pvp.rs` today), mirroring the existing practice-XP exemption and this milestone's own stated Trust design intent (ADR-0019's 2026-08-02 amendment).
- **EG2-8:** `accrue_quality_time(ctx, monster_id)` SHALL be a shared helper, added to `evals/no-idle-accrual.eval.mjs`'s `GROWTH_WRITERS` allowlist, called DIRECTLY (never transitively) from exactly five reducers, all landing in this slice's single PR (not split across parallel work): `care`, `train`, `essence_train`, `write_back_battle_results`, and the existing player-triggered movement-intent reducer (the non-scheduled move-input reducer — exact symbol confirmed at implementation time, distinct from the scheduled `movement_tick` driver).
- **EG2-9 (hard invariant, proof-of-teeth):** `accrue_quality_time` SHALL NEVER be called, directly or transitively, from any scheduled reducer — a dedicated companion test pins this (mirrors `no-idle-accrual.eval.mjs` Check B's scheduled-reducer callgraph scan, which already forbids any `GROWTH_WRITERS` entry from being reachable from a scheduled reducer).
- **EG2-10 (proof-of-teeth):** a wrong-item application of `consume_crystalized_essence` (an item with `essence_affinity: None`, or one that ALSO satisfies an unrelated gate) SHALL be rejected with the item NOT consumed; an unowned-monster application SHALL be rejected — reducer-security parity with `evolve`'s existing test shape.
- Touches: `server-module/src/{evolution,raising,battle,movement}.rs` (+ their `_tests.rs`), `server-module/src/lib.rs` (reducer registration). **SERIAL after EG1**; disjoint from EG3/EG4 once EG1 lands (`touches:` non-overlapping).

### EG3 — MEDIUM: content authoring (`evolutions.ron`/`fusion.ron` successor + species migration)

Evidence: `game-core/content/evolutions.ron` and `game-core/content/fusion.ron` are today single files (non-glob); every other content type (`abilities/`, `items/`, `shops/`, `species/`, `npcs/`, ...) is already a glob-loaded ADR-0057 directory.

- **EG3-1:** `game-core/content/evolution_paths/000-core.ron` (new glob-loaded ADR-0057 directory) SHALL REPLACE `evolutions.ron` and `fusion.ron` entirely — both deleted. Every authored entry SHALL include an explicit, unique `edge_id: u32` (EG1-4/EG1-12) — content authoring assigns it directly, matching `species_id`'s existing convention, never left to a DB default.
- **EG3-2:** species 6 (Steamveil), orphaned by fusion's removal, SHALL gain exactly two new fan-in `EvolutionPath` edges: from species 1 (level 20, requiring Water essence 120) and from species 2 (level 20, requiring Fire essence 120) — complementary essence types, directly realizing Drew's own fire+air illustrative example, and the ONE content fix every converged-design candidate independently reached.
- **EG3-3:** species 1's rare branch (previously `Bond(200)` alone, no level component) SHALL migrate to `min_level: 1`, `essence: [(Fire, 150)]`, `min_trust_tier: Some(Trusting)` — the relative-difficulty-preserving mapping (200/255 ≈ .78 → Trusting, not Devoted), not silently dropped.
- **EG3-4:** species 21→23 (previously `Bond(180)` alone) SHALL migrate identically in shape: `min_level: 1`, `essence: []`, `min_trust_tier: Some(Trusting)` (180/255 ≈ .71 → Trusting).
- **EG3-5:** species 20→22 (Umbraquill→Venumbra) SHALL be authored as a PLAIN `min_level: 22` gate, NO Trust component — re-verification against the live `evolutions.ron` (not the first design draft, which mis-tagged this edge) found this branch was never Bond-gated; only species 1 and species 21 actually used Bond.
- **EG3-6:** items 4 (Tidewell Shard) and 5 (Emberheart Cinder) — B2's still-unshipped trigger items — SHALL gain `essence_affinity: Some(Water)`/`essence_amount: 100` and `essence_affinity: Some(Fire)`/`essence_amount: 100` respectively (replacing their discrete `Item(id)` trigger role), validated mutually exclusive with `train_stat`/`cure_status` (rule R9), and SHALL be stocked in shop 1 at `buy_price ≈ 500` (`sell_price: 200`, the documented 40% convention) in the SAME PR as EG2-4 (`consume_crystalized_essence`) — never buyable-but-inert.
- **EG3-7:** species 7→9 and species 8→10 (the common branches) SHALL be re-authored as `min_level: 18`/`20` respectively, `essence: []` — zero species-data changes (base stats/BST bands untouched), only the incoming edges re-authored.

## 4. Decisions (escalated to Drew per the ADR-0019 amendment; defaults stated so the runner isn't blocked)

Six items genuinely reversible-only-at-real-cost — two where reviewers split during the original B1–B6 synthesis, two surfaced by later review, and two more (marked 2026-08-02) surfaced by a post-ceremony review once Drew supplied real full-roster scale numbers (~500 species, 7–9 tiers, 3–10 evolutions/species) the original ceremony didn't have. All six are implemented per their stated DEFAULT in this spec (§2) so the build is never blocked; all six remain open for Drew's explicit confirmation before this milestone is considered fully closed.

- **Bond retention.** DEFAULT (implemented): retire entirely — its investment-accumulator role and both its evolution-gate uses fold into Trust (majority 4-of-6 design position; Drew's directive names exactly three history stats and never mentions Bond). Escalated because Bond is a currently-shipped, player-visible stat (raising screen, evolution gates) and reversing the deletion after implementation is a real re-migration, not a config flag.
- **Evolution-graph visibility / spoiler-gating.** DEFAULT (implemented): fully public/visible to every player from day one — the Cyber-Sleuth-style, no-spoilers requirements screen every one of the six independent candidate designs defaulted to without much debate (EG4-1). Escalated because retrofitting spoiler-gating after players are used to full transparency is a bigger lift than deciding it now.
- **Strict tier-per-edge advancement.** DEFAULT (implemented): every evolution edge requires `to_species.tier == from_species.tier + 1` exactly, no skipping (R5) — stronger than Drew's literal directive, which only requires monsters not downgrade to a lower tier. Escalated because this is a DAG-by-construction engineering choice (defensible, not exploit-driven) that goes beyond what Drew explicitly asked for, and — like the other items here — reversing it after content is authored against strict +1 edges is a real re-authoring cost, not a config flag. **Stakes raised 2026-08-02:** at the real target (7–9+ tiers, ~500 species), this rule means EVERY species needs an unbroken chain of authored content at every intermediate tier or a monster gets permanently stuck one step short of its next evolution — a much bigger content-authoring commitment than it looked like against the ceremony's original 4-tier assumption. Worth Drew's explicit confirmation before content authoring scales up, not just a documented default.
- **Full essence reset on evolution vs. a shipped-content collision risk.** DEFAULT (implemented): all 8 essence pools zero unconditionally on every evolution, matching Drew's own phrasing that essence is "spent" by the edge trigger (ADR-0019's 2026-08-02 amendment). Escalated because shipped content creates a concrete near-term collision: species 7 has two simultaneously-eligible branches (7→9, `essence: []`, vs 7→30, `essence: [(Water, 100)]`) where taking the free branch silently destroys a banked Water stockpile with zero pre-evolve warning (that UI is separately deferred, §6) — Drew should confirm the full-reset default is still wanted given this concrete, already-shipped example, not just the abstract mechanic.
- **Tier cap value (added 2026-08-02).** DEFAULT (implemented): `Species.tier <= 5` (R11) — six tiers total (0–5). This is EXPLICITLY PROVISIONAL, not a final answer: the ceremony's original `<= 3` was sized against a 4-tier assumption with no real roster numbers behind it; Drew's actual stated target is ~500 species across 7–9 tiers, which is HIGHER than this provisional cap. `5` was chosen deliberately as an interim value to unblock content authoring now, not as the final count — revisit and likely raise again (R11 is a one-line, cheap-to-change rule; the `tier: u8` column itself already has headroom to 255, so raising the cap later is not a schema change) once the actual final tier count is confirmed, and before content authoring approaches the current cap.
- **Trust-tier granularity ceiling (added 2026-08-02).** DEFAULT (implemented): `TrustTier` stays a 4-variant closed enum (`Wary`/`Neutral`/`Trusting`/`Devoted`). Escalated because at 7–9+ species tiers, 4 Trust tiers cannot provide a distinct Trust-gate threshold for every tier transition even if every transition wanted one (unlike `quality_time_tier: u8`, which is an open numeric scale with no such ceiling). The default assumption is that NOT every tier transition needs a Trust gate — some can be essence/level-only — so 4 variants may simply be enough breadth for however many transitions actually want one. Not verified against real content yet (only 2 of the 9 currently-shipped edges use a Trust gate). Revisit if content authoring finds itself wanting more than 4 distinct Trust thresholds across the real roster; the fix (more `TrustTier` variants, or switching Trust to a numeric percentage like Nutrition) is a bigger change than the tier-cap fix above and shouldn't be done speculatively.

## 5. Validation rules (successor to `validate_evolution_fusion`)

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| R1 | Well-formed; no duplicate `(from_species, to_species)` pair. Enforced at the content-integrity gate AND, per EG1-4 (added 2026-08-02), by a DB-level unique index — not offline-gate-only. This is also what makes `to_species` alone a sufficient, unambiguous key for `evolve()` (EG2-1); see EG1-12's contingency note if this rule is ever relaxed. |
| R2 | No self-evolution. |
| R3 | No dangling species/item/affinity references. |
| R4 | No vacuous path: reject if `min_level <= 1` AND `essence` empty AND all three history-stat gates are `None`. |
| R5 | Tier monotonicity: `to_species.tier == from_species.tier + 1`, strict, no skipping. |
| R6 | Derived-forms-not-wild: no `to_species` may appear in any encounter table. |
| R7 | `essence.len() <= 3` per path (legibility cap — the requirements panel depends on paths staying readable as one card). |
| R8 | Fan-in explicitly permitted — a POSITIVE pinned test (two paths, same `to_species`, different `from_species`, both validate cleanly), not merely un-forbidden. |
| R9 | `ItemDef.essence_affinity.is_some()` ⇒ `train_stat` and `cure_status` must both be `None`. |
| R10 | Universal reachability: every species with `tier > 0` must be the `to_species` of at least one path — the structural fix that makes another Steamveil-class orphan impossible to ship silently. |
| R11 | Tier cap: `Species.tier <= 5` (**PROVISIONAL, revised 2026-08-02** — the ceremony's original `<= 3` was sized against a 4-tier assumption made without knowing the real target; the actual full-roster target is ~500 species across 7–9 tiers, and `5` was chosen as a stated provisional value, not a final confirmed count — revisit before content authoring reaches the top of this range); combined with R5's strict +1 monotonicity, this is the structural enforcement the 2026-08-02 ADR amendment names. |
| R12 | `edge_id` well-formedness (added 2026-08-02): unique across all `EvolutionPath` content, and — like `species_id` — append-only (an `edge_id` already shipped in content SHALL NEVER be reused or reassigned to a different edge across content revisions, even if the original edge is later removed). |

Because `evolve()` takes an explicit player-chosen target rather than resolving a first-match-wins order, the "dominated/subset requirement makes a sibling branch unreachable" bug class **cannot occur under this design** — no dominance-ordering rule is needed.

## 6. Out of scope / deferred (noted, not blocking, not sent to Drew)

- All numeric constants (essence amounts, cooldowns, thresholds, tier bands) are playtest-tunable placeholders (EG2-3/EG2-4/EG2-7's proposed values, EG3's proposed RON amounts) — a dedicated balance/playtest pass owns final tuning. This does NOT cover `trust_tier_of`'s Bayesian-smoothing constant `K = 10` or its formula shape (EG1-6) — that is a structural design choice from Drew's directive, fixed, not a placeholder number.
- Rare-Candy-style tier-skip currency, and a future "combine two same-species monsters" mechanic reusing A0-style math — explicitly not built, noted as plausible future levers only, per the charter's caution against gratuitous invention.
- PvP essence/Trust/Quality-Time settlement — superseded by EG2-7's revised scope: essence, Trust, and Quality-Time credit are now practice- AND PvP-exempt (wild battles only), closing the collusion-farming vector a PvP-inclusive design would open (§2 EG2-7). This makes the prior side-A/side-B essence-parity question moot — neither side settles essence through PvP. PvP's XP (a separate growth resource, unaffected by this amendment) remains deferred for side-B per ADR-0109 D10, tracked there, not here.
- Raising state (essence, Trust, Quality-Time, level, Nutrition) transfers wholesale on trade with no reset — a pre-raised, evolve-ready monster is a tradeable commodity that lets a buyer bypass every pacing gate for that monster. Noted as a known gap, not solved here; a trade-time reset or discount needs its own reducer-level design pass in `trading.rs`, out of scope for this milestone.
- Trust's exact favorable/unfavorable taxonomy beyond the MVP pair (care success, capped battle-win, faint) — Drew's own directive calls these "suggestions," open to a later content pass.
- Pre-evolve UI warning about full essence loss — a real UX beat, out of scope for this ceremony (belongs to the `uxd` milestones).
- `evolved_via_edge_id: Option<u32>` — C's retired lineage intent's one plausible cheap remnant; not built now (§3). References the durable `edge_id` (EG1-4/EG1-12), never the auto_inc `path_id`.
- **Multi-eligible-path UX at full roster scale (added 2026-08-02).** EG4-2's "present all eligible paths, never auto-resolve" requirement has only been demonstrated against 2–3 simultaneously-authored branches (species 7, 20/21 examples). At the real target (up to 10 outgoing edges per non-top-tier species), nothing about the design breaks structurally, but a 10-way choice card and a 10-row progress panel is a materially different UI problem than a 2–3-way one and hasn't been designed against. Deferred to when content authoring actually reaches species with that many branches — not blocking EG1–EG5.
- A dedicated EV-reversal ("Pomeg Berry")-style item and a food-economy tuning pass — flagged by the design as a real risk (making rare food items the *sole* Nutrition path is a departure from nearly every surveyed precedent), not solved here.
