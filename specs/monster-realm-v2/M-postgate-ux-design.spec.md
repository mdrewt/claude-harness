# Spec: M-postgate-ux-design — the three larger deferred UX design questions (responsive viewport · shop-via-NPC interaction · unified overlay IA / main menu)

**Status:** **DESIGNED** 2026-07-25 (converged via a brainstorm→debate→judge→synthesize multi-agent pass, Drew-directed) · **implementation post-gate provisional** (built after a cleaner second playtest read, same as the rest of Phase D — this spec delivers the *designs*, it does not unblock the *build* ahead of the gate) · **Owner:** Drew · **Decision:** ADR reserved at build time, per slice
**Stack:** spacetimedb-game · **Project:** monster-realm · **Depends on:** M-playtest-a/b/c/c.5/d CLOSED; playtest gate run 2026-07-25.
**Spawned by:** `playtest-gate-decision-2026-07-25.md` §8 (the three "real but larger, separate design questions … each deserves its own sizing pass" that were deliberately NOT bundled into the two hardening milestones). This is that sizing pass.

## 1. Problem / intent

Drew's 2026-07-25 closed playtest surfaced three UX friction sources that are genuinely *larger design questions*, not quick affordance fixes, and were correctly kept out of `M-postgate-ux-hardening`:
1. **Responsive viewport scaling** — the canvas resizes but the world does not; retina blur, no min/max zoom, tiny zones stranded top-left.
2. **Shop-via-NPC interaction** — the shop opens from a world-unconnected global `KeyG`; a playtester expected to walk up to a shopkeeper and interact.
3. **"I'd expect one main menu with submenus"** — ~15 memorized hotkeys instead of a discoverable menu.

Each was designed by an independent multi-agent convergence pass (3 divergent brainstorm lenses → adversarial debate → rubric judge → synthesis → convergence critic, looping to spec-readiness). All three converged; the winning designs and their EARS criteria are below. Every claim in each design was grounded against `master` @ `3d02b38` and cites `file:line`.

## 2. How to read this spec

Each slice carries the converged design's **key decisions**, **EARS acceptance criteria**, **touches** (grounded), **out-of-scope**, and **open questions for Drew** (each with a recommended default so the slice is buildable without a blocking round-trip). The designs commit to a direction; the open questions are *confirm-not-block* product/feel calls. ADR numbers are **reserved at build time** (`adr_next_free=146` as of 2026-07-25) — do not pre-assign here.

## 3. Slices

### uxd1 — Responsive viewport scaling: DPR-correct, device-integer crisp, fill-model follow-camera

**Intent.** Add a render-**edge-only** responsive viewport that keeps the resolution-agnostic integer-tile grid intact. The whole game-core/netcode pipeline stays fractional-tile (`interpolation.ts:30-33`, `slideClock.ts:42-48`, `renderResolver.ts`) with `TILE_PX` applied only at the leaf (`characterView.ts:48-49`), so scaling `stage.scale`/`stage.position`/the Application `resolution` touches nothing stored, sent, predicted, or interpolated — server authority (ADR-0015/0081) and smoothness (ADR-0013) are untouched **by construction**, and the e2e fractional-motion latch (`main.ts:2076-2080`, reads TILE values) stays green.

**Key decisions.**
- **FILL, not letterbox.** Free-scroll follow-camera overworld (ADR-0067) with tiny 10×7 maps; letterbox wastes screen and fights the genre (PvP is a separate battle overlay, so the "identical framing for fairness" rationale does not apply). Large maps scroll to fill; maps smaller than the viewport are **CENTERED** (symmetric `FLOOR_COLOR`), not bar-framed.
- **DPR-correct backing store is Slice A and ships FIRST, ALONE.** Set `resolution=devicePixelRatio` + `autoDensity=true` at init (`world.ts:57-62`, Pixi v8 defaults 1/false = the retina-blur root cause) and re-apply on resize (`world.ts:165`). ~3-line, near-zero-risk retina-crisp win independent of any camera/zoom math; de-risks the rest.
- **Device-integer crisp + fractional-CSS fill (the winning mechanism):** `deviceScale = max(1, round(idealCssScale × dpr))` (integer); `stageScale = deviceScale / dpr` (fractional CSS). Simultaneously fills the viewport AND maps source texels to whole device pixels on any DPR — future-bitmap-art-proof, free today.
- **Min/max zoom is a BOUND, not a knob.** `viewportScale()` targets a shorter-axis tile count, clamped so visible tiles stay in `[MIN_VISIBLE_TILES, MAX_VISIBLE_TILES]`. The user-facing +/−/pinch control + persistence + help copy are **DEFERRED** to a later slice and, when built, route through the overlay registry (uxd3) — never a new open-coded `main.ts` hotkey.
- **Small-map centering is OWNED here.** `camera.ts` gains a per-axis branch: scroll-clamp when `mapPx >= effectiveViewport` (unchanged), CENTER at `-(effViewport - mapPx)/2` when smaller. ⚠ This **breaks existing green camera teeth** (`camera.test.ts` C1b lines 107-119 assert `(0,0)`; property lower-bounds `off>=0` at 229/231) — those are rewritten AND new centering teeth added, keeping large-map scroll assertions as a regression guard.
- **New pure `render/viewport.ts`** owns `viewportScale()` + `worldToScreen`/`screenToWorld`, mirroring the `camera.ts`/`resizeWiring.ts` functional-core seam (ADR-0014) with fast-check teeth (ADR-0010). `worldToScreen`/`screenToWorld` are the exact primitive a future interaction-prompt (see uxd2) / mobile tap-to-move will consume.
- **`resizeWiring` seam WIDENED** to thread `devicePixelRatio` (structural field, read at fire-time) so it stays node-testable and handles runtime DPR change (dragging between retina/non-retina monitors).
- **DOM overlays unchanged** — they stay `position:fixed; inset:0` over the full window (`boxView.ts:32` and peers), anchored to the WINDOW not the scaled world, so they never mis-align. Asserted, not left implicit.
- **Shipping slice does NOT round `stage.position` to the device grid** (preserves ADR-0013 sub-tile slide smoothness); per-frame device-grid rounding for pixel-perfect scrolled *bitmap* art is a named deferral for when real tiles land.

**Acceptance criteria (EARS).**
- WHEN the WorldRenderer initializes on a display with `devicePixelRatio` dpr, THE renderer SHALL create the Pixi Application with `resolution = dpr` and `autoDensity = true`, so the backing store is `cssW*dpr × cssH*dpr` device pixels while the canvas CSS box remains `cssW × cssH`.
- WHEN a window resize fires AND the live `devicePixelRatio` differs from the value used at the last layout, THE resize path SHALL re-read it, set `app.renderer.resolution` to the new dpr, and resize the backing store to current CSS dimensions.
- WHERE the shorter viewport axis at the chosen scale would show fewer than `MIN_VISIBLE_TILES` or more than `MAX_VISIBLE_TILES`, THE `viewportScale` core SHALL clamp `stageScale` so the shorter-axis visible-tile count lies within `[MIN_VISIBLE_TILES, MAX_VISIBLE_TILES]`.
- WHEN computing scale for dpr, THE `viewportScale` core SHALL return `deviceScale = max(1, round(idealCssScale*dpr))` (integer) and `stageScale = deviceScale/dpr`, so one source texel maps to a whole number of device pixels for dpr ∈ {1,2,3} while the fractional CSS scale still fills the viewport.
- WHERE the map in scaled source pixels (`mapTiles*TILE_PX*stageScale`) is smaller than the viewport along an axis, THE FollowCamera SHALL center the map on that axis (`offset = -(effectiveViewport - mapPx)/2`) rather than pinning top-left.
- WHERE the map in scaled source pixels is `>=` the viewport along an axis, THE FollowCamera SHALL scroll-clamp that axis to `[0, mapPx - effectiveViewport]` centered on the player's tile, so no pixels outside the map edge are shown (unchanged large-map behavior).
- WHEN the renderer applies the camera each frame, THE renderer SHALL set `stage.scale = stageScale` and `stage.position = (-offsetX*stageScale, -offsetY*stageScale)`, such that `worldToScreen(ownPlayerTileCenter)` equals the viewport center within one device pixel across every supported scale and dpr.
- WHILE any DOM overlay is visible, THE overlay SHALL remain fixed to the full window viewport (`position:fixed; inset:0`) above the canvas, independent of `stageScale` and dpr.
- WHEN the game-core tile-space pipeline resolves entity positions, THE viewport scaling SHALL leave every stored/sent/predicted/interpolated value unchanged (TILE_PX and stageScale applied only at the render leaf), so server authority and ADR-0013 smoothness hold and the e2e fractional-motion latch stays green.
- WHERE generated placeholder or future bitmap textures are drawn at a fractional CSS `stageScale`, THE texture SHALL use `nearest` scaleMode so an integer `deviceScale` yields texel-crisp sprites, with no visible degradation of the current rounded-rect placeholders at scale 1.
- WHEN the own entity slides sub-tile across a full `STEP_MS`, THE resulting on-screen world position SHALL advance monotonically without per-frame reversal (device-scaling introduces no own-entity judder the tile-value e2e latch cannot see).
- WHEN the resize handler is installed, THE handler SHALL read `devicePixelRatio` from the injected window at fire-time (not capture-time) and thread it to the renderer alongside `innerWidth/innerHeight`.

**Touches:** `client/src/render/{world.ts,config.ts,camera.ts,camera.test.ts,resizeWiring.ts,resizeWiring.test.ts,placeholderAssets.ts}` · NEW `client/src/render/viewport.ts` (+ `viewport.test.ts`: deviceScale-integer, visible-tile bounds, worldToScreen-center, screenToWorld round-trip) · `client/src/main.ts` (thread a dpr provider into `installResizeHandler`; render-loop tile values unchanged; NO new hotkey) · NEW ADR (reserved at build). Pure-client render edge — no server/schema/#view surface.

**Out of scope:** user-adjustable zoom UI (+/−/pinch + persistence + help copy — deferred, routes through uxd3 when built); touch/mobile input handlers (the `screenToWorld` seam ships for a future milestone to consume, no listeners wired); M23 accessibility; per-frame device-grid rounding for scrolled bitmap art; the overlay-registry itself (that's uxd3); real art/spritesheets (standing M4 deferral — only placeholder `scaleMode` changes); letterbox models.

**Open questions for Drew (recommended defaults in the design):**
- **Zoom feel numbers** — default shorter-axis target ~11 tiles, `MIN/MAX_VISIBLE_TILES` = 7 / 16? Sets on-screen sprite/tile size across phones→4K. *Feel call; EARS use them symbolically so remain testable at any value.*
- **Small zones** — show the WHOLE 10×7 zone CENTERED (design's committed direction), or zoom further to fill edge-to-edge (can't then see the whole zone)? *Confirm the centering matches intent.*
- **Phasing** — ship bounded auto-zoom now, defer the +/−/pinch knob (recommended, YAGNI)?
- **Touch tap-to-move** — in scope for any near-term milestone? If purely long-horizon, the seam still ships but no touch work is scheduled.

**Residual (confirm-not-block):** the visible-tile clamp × sub-viewport-map interaction (e.g. a 7-tall zone under an 11-tile target) is handled by the center-when-smaller branch; worth an explicit composition tooth so clamp + centering are shown to compose.

### uxd2 — Shop-via-NPC: a context-sensitive interact system (server-anchored `NpcInteraction` enum + generalized `KeyT` + on-world prompt)

**DELIVERED (2026-07-31, ADR-0161, PR pending).** Reconciliations vs literal spec: **(a)** GREET-THEN-SHOP implemented per Drew's direction — AC "nearest interactable is an NPC with Shop(shopId), THE client SHALL open the shop overlay ... without calling any reducer" satisfied via greeting hop (KeyT sends `talk` reducer for Dialogue, enum-derived Shop button dismisses greeting then opens bound shop overlay with no reducer call); **(b)** "WHEN KeyG is pressed..." AC SUPERSEDED by Drew's "Do not keep" — `KeyG`/`KeyH` handlers and helpModel rows removed, default first-shop/first-location model arms preserved and regression-pinned; **(c)** heal overlay binding is VIEW-only (`buildHealViewModelForLocation`), heal_party SEND retains first-location default (residual: bind send when second heal location exists). Note: `touches:` under-enumeration found at build (rowConvert.ts, connection.ts mirror, dialogueContent.ts, dialogueView.ts, helpModel.ts+test, main.wiring key lists, world.ts screenFor) — recorded in PR touches-delta; trade.spec.ts KeyG/KeyH press blocks now vacuously green (follow-up flagged).

**Intent.** One context-sensitive interact action: based on the nearest in-range interactable, the interact key opens the right thing — talk to a Dialogue NPC, shop at a Shop NPC, heal at a heal target. Matches the playtester's "walk up to a shopkeeper and interact" mental model and ADR-0069's "NPCs may offer healing at their location" intent.

**Key decisions.**
- **Data model = server-anchored SSOT.** Add an exhaustive `NpcInteraction{Dialogue, Shop(u32), Heal(u32)}` `SpacetimeType` enum (`game-core/src/types.rs`, cfg-gated like the enums at `types.rs:21-89`, `Default=Dialogue`), as an additive `#[serde(default)]` field on `NpcDef` (`game-core/src/content.rs:1114`) and a matching column on the public `npc` table (`server-module/src/schema.rs:361`), threaded through `npc_row_from_def`. Mirrors the nullable-enum precedent `cure_status: Option<StatusKind>` (`schema.rs:143`). Chosen over a client-only role map (drifts, forecloses hardening) and over a bare `Option<u32> shop_id` (repo prizes exhaustive enums; the enum unifies heal-on-NPC without a 2nd column). It is the ONLY shape that lets `buy()`/`heal_party()` be server-proximity-hardened later.
- **Seed minimalism.** Define all three variants for compile-time match safety, but SEED only `Dialogue` (elder_oak keeps the serde default) + one `Shop` NPC now. No speculative healer content.
- **Resolver = generalize the proven pure `nearestTalkableNpcId`** (`dialogueModel.ts:39-57`; same-zone, min-Manhattan, lowest-id, range 2) into `nearestInteractable(...)` returning a discriminated descriptor `{kind:'dialogue',npcEntityId} | {kind:'shop',npcEntityId,shopId} | {kind:'heal',locationId}`. Determinism preserved and extended for MIXED kinds (distance asc → NPC-anchored before tile → lowest id). Range = `CLIENT_INTERACT_RANGE` (=2).
- **Heal-via-TILE graft.** Heal tiles are already public + positioned + in the store (`schema.rs:425-436`, `store.healLocations()`), so the shared resolver treats them as heal interactables with ZERO server heal work — heal value ships in the same client slice as shop, even before any Heal-variant NPC exists.
- **Dispatch = MUTATE the existing `KeyT` handler** (`main.ts:830-873`), keeping its 14-way "no overlay visible" guard. Dialogue → unchanged `talk` reducer; Shop → open shop overlay bound to `shopId`, no reducer; Heal → open heal overlay bound to `locationId`, no reducer. Adds **NO new hotkey and NO 14th mutual-exclusion block** (rejects a `KeyF`) — the overlay registry (uxd3) stays independent.
- **select-by-id migration.** Add `shopId`/`locationId`-scoped variants to `buildShopViewModel` (`shopModel.ts:59` hard-selects first shop today) and the heal model (`healModel.ts:31`). `KeyG`/`KeyH` keep passing the default (first shop/heal), regression-guarded, so their pre-milestone behavior is byte-preserved.
- **Discovery prompt = pure view-model + DOM element.** `interactPrompt(...)` → `{visible, actionWord:'Talk'|'Shop'|'Heal', keyGlyph:'T', anchorTileX, anchorTileY} | null`, plus a `#interact-prompt` DOM node positioned each frame via the pure exported `FollowCamera.offsetFor` (`camera.ts:9`) + TILE_PX (anchor screen-x = `targetTileX*TILE_PX - offset.x + 0.5-tile-center`). **NOT a Pixi world-space label** — `world.ts:12` imports only Application/Container/Graphics (no text pipeline; un-node-testable, z-order-coupled).
- **Validator as a SEPARATE function.** Add `validate_npc_interactions(npcs, shops, heal_locations)` cross-referencing every `Shop(id)`/`Heal(id)`, called alongside `validate_npc_content` at `sync_content` — do NOT extend `validate_npc_content`'s 6-arg signature (~40 test call sites depend on it). Independently falsifiable (ADR-0010).
- **Reducers untouched in MVP.** `buy` (`economy.rs:102`, already global) and `heal_party` (`raising.rs:284`, already zone-checked) stay byte-identical — no shipped capability removed, no reducer-change e2e can regress. Global buy/heal remains until a documented Phase-2 proximity-hardening gate.
- **Content placement = zone 1, NOT frozen zone 0.** Seed the shopkeeper in zone 1 "Tideglass Cove", reachable via the existing zone-0 `(5,5)`→zone-1 warp. ⚠ This is the highest-risk detail: `dialogue.spec.ts:195-217` walks toward ANY non-own character in zone 0 and presses `KeyT` expecting `#dialogue-overlay` — a generalized `KeyT` near a service NPC in zone 0 would open the SHOP overlay and flake the dialogue e2e. Zone 1 sidesteps both that AND pt-d3's frozen-zone-0 flake budget. The shopkeeper needs a valid non-null `dialogue_tree_id` (validator step 3, `content.rs:1272-1280`) → author a minimal inert greeting tree.
- **Ship gates.** `CONTENT_VERSION` bump (`server-module/src/lib.rs`; ADR-0054 silent-skip trap), bindings regen, `just knowledge` + `just adr-digest`, and a clean BSATN republish (ADR-0103: the additive `SpacetimeType` column widens a PUBLIC row → all subscribers re-sync; run the additive-column/bindings-drift + schema-snapshot-parser gate).

**Acceptance criteria (EARS).**
- WHEN the player presses `KeyT` AND no overlay is visible AND the nearest same-zone interactable within `CLIENT_INTERACT_RANGE` is an NPC whose interaction is `Dialogue`, THE client SHALL send the existing `talk` reducer for that entity and open no other overlay.
- WHEN … the nearest interactable is an NPC with `Shop(shopId)`, THE client SHALL open the shop overlay bound to `shopId` without calling any reducer.
- WHEN … the nearest interactable is a heal target with `locationId`, THE client SHALL open the heal overlay bound to `locationId` without calling any reducer.
- WHERE two or more interactables are within range in the player's zone, THE `nearestInteractable` resolver SHALL select deterministically by ascending Manhattan distance, then NPC-anchored before tile-anchored, then lowest entity/location id.
- IF no interactable is within range in the player's zone, THEN THE interact key SHALL no-op AND the on-world prompt SHALL be hidden.
- WHILE any overlay is visible, THE interact key SHALL NOT open a new overlay AND the on-world prompt SHALL remain hidden.
- WHEN a nearest interactable exists AND no overlay is visible, THE client SHALL render `#interact-prompt` anchored above the target tile via `FollowCamera.offsetFor` + TILE_PX, showing the action word (Talk|Shop|Heal) and the interact-key glyph.
- WHERE an `NpcDef` declares `Shop(id)`/`Heal(id)`, THE content validator SHALL fail the build IF `id` does not reference an existing `shop_row`/`heal_location_row`.
- WHERE an `NpcDef` omits the interaction field in RON, THE deserializer SHALL default it to `Dialogue` so all pre-existing NPC content stays valid.
- WHEN `KeyG` is pressed AND no overlay is visible, THE client SHALL open the shop overlay bound to the first shop, preserving exact pre-milestone `KeyG` behavior.
- WHERE this milestone ships, THE `buy` and `heal_party` reducers SHALL remain byte-identical to their pre-milestone baseline (proximity hardening deferred).
- WHEN the player is within range (Manhattan `<= 2`) of the zone-1 shopkeeper, THE prompt SHALL read "Shop" AND `KeyT` SHALL make `#shop-overlay` visible; WHEN 3 tiles away, the prompt SHALL be hidden AND `KeyT` SHALL not open it.
- WHERE the shopkeeper is seeded, THE content SHALL place it in zone 1 with no Shop/Heal interactable within range of the dialogue e2e's zone-0 walk path, AND the existing dialogue e2e SHALL remain green.
- WHERE an NPC declares `Shop(id)`/`Heal(id)`, THE `npc` table row SHALL carry that interaction after `sync_content` so the client resolver reads it from the store (no client-side role map).

**Touches:** `game-core/src/types.rs` (NEW enum), `game-core/src/content.rs` (additive `NpcDef.interaction` + NEW `validate_npc_interactions`), `game-core/src/lib.rs` (re-exports), `server-module/src/schema.rs` (`npc.interaction` column), `server-module/src/content.rs` (thread + call validator), `server-module/src/lib.rs` (`CONTENT_VERSION`), `game-core/content/npcs/000-core.ron` (zone-1 shopkeeper), `game-core/content/dialogue_trees/000-core.ron` (inert greeting), `client/src/net/store.ts` (`StoreNpcRow.interaction` + `upsertNpc`), `client/src/ui/dialogueModel.ts`→generalized `nearestInteractable` (a NEW `interactModel.ts` is acceptable), `client/src/ui/{shopModel,healModel}.ts` (select-by-id variants), `client/src/main.ts` (`KeyT` dispatch; `KeyG/KeyH` default; wire `#interact-prompt` into the render loop), `client/src/render/camera.ts` (CONSUME `offsetFor`, no change), NEW `client/e2e/shop-npc.spec.ts` (zone-0→warp→zone-1 shopkeeper→prompt→`KeyT`→`#shop-overlay`), node tests (resolver range/zone/kind-precedence/tiebreak; prompt anchor+label; select-by-id; `KeyG`-still-first-shop regression), validator teeth (bad `Shop(id)`/`Heal(id)` fail the build). NEW ADR (reserved at build). **Full-stack additive slice → SERIAL vs any concurrent schema-touching slice.**

**Out of scope:** server proximity-hardening of `buy()`/`heal_party()` (separate Phase-2 gate; MVP leaves them global — documented, not oversight); seeding a Heal-on-NPC nurse (the variant is defined for exhaustiveness; heal ships via the existing tile); a first-class non-NPC interactable-tile registry for signs/warps (YAGNI); the overlay-registry unification (uxd3); a new `KeyF`; a Pixi over-head label; M23.

**Open questions for Drew (recommended defaults in the design):**
- **Shopkeeper flow** — `KeyT` opens the shop DIRECTLY (matches "walk up and shop", simplest — design default), or GREET-THEN-SHOP via a one-node dialogue with Shop/Leave (more immersive, uses the validator-required tree, adds a hop)?
  - Answer provided by Drew: GREET-THEN-SHOP

  - Answer provided by Drew: Recommendations accepted.
- **Shopkeeper placement in zone 1** — at the warp landing tile (found immediately) or deeper (exploration reward)? And its greeting line?
  - Answer provided by Drew: Deeper, with a placeholder greeting line of "Hello, customer!"

**Residuals (confirm-not-block):** the AC path (direct-open) is the labeled default vs the still-open greet-then-shop question — reconcile at build; a Shop NPC still needs a live `dialogue_tree_id` so the seeded greeting is dead content under direct-open (acceptable, note the coupling).

### uxd3 — Unified overlay IA: a registry-backed two-level main menu that **subsumes** the parked `M-postgate-overlay-registry`

**Intent.** Replace ~15 memorized hotkeys with one discoverable two-level menu (categories → leaves), opened by a pinned front-door key, with quick-hotkeys preserved as shortcuts. This milestone **SUBSUMES** the parked `M-postgate-overlay-registry`: that slice has no spec and was never built (parked at ptc5c Decision B; gate-decision §9 says the main-menu redesign "folds into it when eventually scoped"). So there is no separately-landed substrate — this milestone IS the scoping, delivering BOTH the registry substrate and the menu IA in one, and **retiring** the parked entry so no duplicate registry work remains queued.

**Key decisions.**
- **Front-door PINNED to `KeyM`** (mnemonic "Menu"; verified UNBOUND in the keydown handler; no `KEY_DIR`/letter/`?` collision, no browser default). **Escape is deliberately NOT overloaded** — it stays a pure close/back key, so mashing Escape never surprises the player with a menu. `KeyM` is added to the `helpModel` CONTROLS SSOT ("M — Open menu") so it self-documents.
- **Self-owned fallback + decided ux1 handoff.** `KeyM` requires zero DOM, so this milestone NEVER blocks on `M-postgate-ux-hardening` ux1. The persistent CLICK front-door is a SINGLE corner element (`#menu-launcher`, same fixed-corner footprint as `#build-stamp` at `index.html:87-90` — explicitly NOT an always-on rail) that **doubles as ux1-1's "Press ? for controls & help" hint**. Exactly ONE corner affordance may exist. Sequencing: ux1 lands first → this reuses/relabels its badge as the launcher host; this lands first → it ships `#menu-launcher` and marks ux1-1 satisfied-by-launcher. Either order = one element + a working menu.
- **Mechanism = an explicit `canOpen(targetId, currentVisibleId)` modality reducer, NOT a blind `hideAll`.** Decisive: only `canOpen` reproduces the gate's guard-vs-hide split; a blind `hideAll` regresses the ptc5c/ADR-0139 fix and desyncs the server conversation row.
- **Modality tiers map the ACTUAL gate exactly** (verified `main.wiring.test.ts:1612-1631,1745-1765`, not the debate's simplified 3-tier): `EXCLUSIVE_TOP = {battleView}` (may force-hide the subset `{help,box,raising,evolution,leaderboard,rename,tradePropose}` per `main.ts:1089-1102`); `HIDE_SWITCH = {boxView,raisingView,evolutionView}` (a sibling open may force-hide another); `GUARD_ONLY = {dialogue,questLog,heal,shop,trade,pvp,leaderboard,rename,tradePropose,help,menu}` (deny over them, NEVER force-hide — force-hiding `dialogueView` would strand the server `player_conversation` row, whose visibility is store-derived at `main.ts:1164-1181` and whose close routes through `dismissDialogue` at `main.ts:965-971`). `errorOverlayView` is NOT a registry member (non-blocking, F8-dismissed, self-re-shows).
- **Two-level IA answers "submenus".** Proposed categories: **Party** (Box B / Raise I / Evolve E / Heal H) · **World** (Quest Log Q / Talk T-contextual) · **Shop & Trade** (Shop G / Incoming Trade U / Offer O-contextual) · **Compete** (PvP P-contextual / Leaderboard L) · **System** (Rename N / Help ? / future Settings slot). `battleView`/`dialogueView` are registry members but NOT menu leaves (context overlays that preempt the menu).
- **`open()` carries PER-ID THUNKS** (each overlay's existing build-VM-and-show closure), never a blind `view.show()`: the view contract is non-uniform (`dialogueView`/`questLog`/`heal` expose `render(vm)` with no `show()`). The registry is a data lookup table (metadata + thunks); all decisions live in the pure `canOpen`/`menuNav` cores (ADR-0014 seam preserved).
- **Unavailable leaves are GREYED/DISABLED, never hidden** — hiding a leaf exactly when the player explores toward its precondition defeats discoverability. `Talk.available()` = `nearestTalkableNpcId(...) !== undefined` (proximity, range 2); `Offer.available()`/`PvP.available()` = a non-empty challengeable/target player list exists (online-player existence, **not** proximity — correcting the SSOT "nearby" copy).
- **Escape semantics = one-press-to-world from a leaf** (preserves today's muscle memory); back-stack ONLY inside the menu (max depth 2: leaf-submenu → Escape/Left pops to category list → Escape closes menu). Escape never spans menu→leaf.
- **`helpView` stays a DISTINCT registry member, NOT folded in** — avoids migrating the `W-HELP-FANOUT-*` cluster and the deliberate PTC2B-9 reconnect-survival asymmetry (`W-HELP-NO-RECONNECT-HIDE`, `main.wiring.test.ts:1489`). The menu only REUSES help's CONTROLS SSOT for labels.
- **SEQUENCING (hard):** lands AFTER netcode-hardening **nh1** (movement-suppression `preventDefault`, `main.ts:1006-1023`) and **nh2** (predictor keyup cancel) — it must never gate those playtest-blocking fixes. When it rewrites the movement-suppression block into `registry.anyVisible()`, it PRESERVES nh1's `e.preventDefault()`.
- **Gate migration is deliberate:** the source-scan `W-OVERLAY-FANOUT-MUTEX` is replaced by a manifest-vs-`*View` completeness scan + a node-level `canOpen` invariant that ports the existing 9-case RED faithfully (removing any `GUARD_ONLY` assignment re-fails it).

**Acceptance criteria (EARS).**
- WHERE no overlay is currently visible, THE `canOpen(targetId)` SHALL return allow for every registered overlay (all 15 members incl. `menuView`).
- WHEN `canOpen(targetId)` is evaluated AND a `GUARD_ONLY` overlay is visible AND `targetId` is not that same overlay, THE registry SHALL return deny AND SHALL NOT force-hide the visible overlay.
- WHEN both `targetId` and the currently-visible overlay are in `{boxView,raisingView,evolutionView}`, THE registry SHALL return allow-with-force-hide of the visible sibling.
- WHEN a battle open is requested AND any overlay in `{help,box,raising,evolution,leaderboard,rename,tradePropose}` is visible, THE `hideAllExcept('battleView')` SHALL hide exactly that subset and leave `battleView` visible (reproducing `refreshBattle`, `main.ts:1089-1102`).
- WHERE a battle overlay is visible, THE `canOpen(targetId)` SHALL return deny for every non-battle target.
- THE manifest SHALL hold exactly one entry per mutual-exclusion overlay (14 existing + `menuView` = 15) AND a completeness test SHALL fail if any `client/src/ui/*View.ts` mutual-exclusion overlay lacks a manifest entry or vice versa, with `errorOverlayView` explicitly excluded.
- WHEN `registry.anyVisible()` is true AND a movement key or Space is pressed, THE keydown handler SHALL suppress movement AND call `e.preventDefault()` before returning (preserving nh1-1).
- WHEN the player presses an overlay hotkey (B/I/E/Q/H/G/U/P/L/N/O or ?) AND `canOpen` returns allow, THE handler SHALL invoke that overlay's build-VM-and-show thunk; IF `canOpen` returns deny, THE handler SHALL no-op while still calling `e.preventDefault()`.
- WHEN Escape is pressed AND `dialogueView` is visible, THE handler SHALL send `dismissDialogue` (never a bare registry hide) so the server `player_conversation` row is cleared.
- WHEN the connection reconnects, THE `onReconnect` handler SHALL hide `renameView/tradeProposeView/shopView/tradeView/pvpView/leaderboardView` AND `menuView`, AND SHALL NOT hide `helpView` (preserving `W-HELP-NO-RECONNECT-HIDE`); `menuView` is hidden because its `available()` gray-out reads store state that goes stale on reset.
- WHEN the player presses `KeyM` AND `canOpen('menuView')` returns allow, THE client SHALL open the menu at the top-level category list; AND THIS SHALL function with NO persistent launcher element present (KeyM is the self-owned, zero-DOM front-door).
- WHERE a single persistent corner launcher exists (`#menu-launcher` here, OR the ux1 badge as host), WHEN clicked AND `canOpen('menuView')` allows, THE client SHALL open the menu; AND at most ONE persistent corner affordance SHALL exist AND a test SHALL fail if a second competing always-on menu/help corner element is added.
- WHERE the menu shows the category list, WHEN the player presses Up/Down or hovers THE menuView SHALL move the category selection, AND WHEN Enter/Right or a click THE menuView SHALL open that category's leaf submenu.
- WHERE the menu shows a leaf submenu, WHEN Escape/Left THE menuView SHALL pop back to the category list; WHERE it shows the top-level list, WHEN Escape THE menuView SHALL close and return control to the world.
- WHEN the player selects a leaf AND its `available()` is true, THE menuView SHALL close itself and route through `registry.open(leafId)`; IF `available()` is false THE menu SHALL render that leaf GREYED/DISABLED and SHALL NOT route — a contextually-unavailable leaf is ALWAYS shown, NEVER hidden.
- THE `Talk` leaf `available()` SHALL be true iff `nearestTalkableNpcId(...) !== undefined`; THE `Offer`/`PvP` leaves `available()` SHALL be true iff at least one challengeable/target player row exists, AND SHALL NOT be a proximity check.
- WHERE an overlay opened via the menu is visible, WHEN Escape THE handler SHALL close it directly to the world in one press AND SHALL NOT re-open the menu.
- THE menu SHALL display each leaf's quick-hotkey label sourced from the `helpModel` CONTROLS SSOT (incl. the new "M — Open menu" row), AND a label test SHALL fail if any displayed key diverges from that SSOT.
- WHEN a battle auto-shows OR the server pushes a dialogue WHILE the menu is visible, THE registry SHALL close the menu and show the context overlay (the menu never occludes a battle or active conversation).
- THE former source-scan `W-OVERLAY-FANOUT-MUTEX` SHALL be replaced by a manifest-completeness test + a node-level `canOpen` invariant reproducing the prior 9-case guard-only RED, such that removing any `GUARD_ONLY` tier assignment re-fails it.
